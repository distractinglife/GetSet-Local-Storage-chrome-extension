# Privacy Policy

GetSet Local Storage, Session Storage & Cookies ("GetSet") is a Chrome extension made by Vinay Dasari.
Last updated: 18 October 2026.

## Summary

GetSet does not collect, sell, share or send any of your data. Everything it handles stays inside your
own browser, on your own computer. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking.

## What GetSet accesses, and why

GetSet only acts when you click one of its buttons, and only on the site in your current tab.

- **Local storage and session storage of the current site.** Read when you click Get, written when you
  click Set, removed when you click Clear.
- **Cookies of the current site.** Only after you allow cookie access for that site in Chrome's
  permission prompt. Read when you click Get, written when you click Set, removed when you click Clear.
  You can withdraw this access at any time on the extension's details page in Chrome.

## Where the copied values are kept

- Copied local and session storage values are saved in the extension's own storage in your browser
  (`chrome.storage.local`), until you click Get again or remove the extension.
- Copied cookies are kept in memory only (`chrome.storage.session`) and are deleted when you close the
  browser.

None of this is uploaded or synced anywhere by GetSet.

## Sensitive data

Storage and cookies can contain login tokens and other sensitive values. GetSet copies them exactly as
you ask, from one site to another in your browser. Use test accounts where you can, and clear the target
site when you are done.

## Third parties

GetSet contains no third-party code, ads or analytics, and shares no data with anyone. The "Give a
review", "Star the repo" and "Report an issue" links simply open those pages in a new tab.
